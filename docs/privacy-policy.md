## The short version

**Your rota never leaves your phone.** The shifts you set up, the days you
change, the notes you write and your reminder settings are stored only in the
app's own private storage on your device. Turnus has no accounts, no sign-up,
no servers, and no way for us to see your data.

The app is free and paid for by a single advert at the bottom of the calendar.
That advert is served by Google AdMob, and **AdMob collects data about your
device**. That is the only data collection in the app, and the rest of this
policy is mostly about it.

## What Turnus itself stores on your device

All of this stays in the app's private storage and is never transmitted:

- Your shift pattern, its start date, and the shift types you define
- Individual days you have changed, and any notes attached to them
- Your reminder preferences

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If you have Android's automatic backup
enabled, Android may include this data in your own Google Drive backup — that is
a function of your device and your Google account, not something Turnus sends
anywhere.

## What we do not do

Turnus contains no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not read your
advertising ID, your device ID, your contacts, your location, your accounts, or
your files. It has no code that uploads your rota anywhere.

## Data collected by Google AdMob

To show adverts, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes:

- Your **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier for advertising)
- Your **IP address**, which indicates your approximate location
- **Device and app information** — model, operating system version, app version
- **Advert interaction data** — which adverts were shown, viewed or tapped

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Its use is governed by
Google's own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and, for
advertising specifically, <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
Turnus asks for your consent before any advert is requested, using Google's
certified consent tool. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time from
**Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices** inside the app.

Outside those regions, adverts may be served without a consent prompt where
local law permits. You can limit advert personalisation in your device settings
under **Settings → Google → Ads**.

## One network request Turnus makes

Turnus fetches a small configuration file from a public address so an advert
slot can be switched off remotely without releasing a new version of the app.

That request contains no identifier and none of your rota data. As with any
internet request, the server receiving it can see the IP address it came from.
The app works normally when the request fails.

## Notifications and alarms

If you turn reminders on, Turnus asks Android for permission to post
notifications and schedules alarms on your device. Those alarms are set and read
entirely on the phone; nothing about your shifts is sent anywhere in order to
remind you.

## Sharing your rota

Exporting your rota produces a calendar file on your device and hands it to
whichever app you choose from the Android share sheet. Turnus does not upload
it. Once you send that file to another app or person, what happens to it is
governed by their handling of it, not ours.

## Children

Turnus is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from
them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or
export your personal data, or to object to its processing.

Because Turnus stores your rota only on your device, you can exercise most of
these yourself: your data is visible in the app, editable in the app, and
deleted entirely when you uninstall or clear the app's data.

For data collected by Google through AdMob, those rights are exercised against
Google. Withdrawing consent in **Settings → Privacy** stops further collection
for advertising where consent is the legal basis.
